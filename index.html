LFM Support Onboarding (Seniors +)

1. Overview

Document Overview & Primary Objective

Legal Faction Management is responsible for overseeing, supporting, and maintaining standards across all legal, government, emergency, public service, and civil legal factions within the community.

As an LFM member, your primary objective is to maintain faction integrity, process administrative requests, and ensure legal roleplay systems function correctly.

2. Team Structure & Responsibilities

Hierarchy & Role Breakdown (Inductor Discussion Guide)

During the induction, the mentor must briefly review the team structure, hierarchy, and core responsibilities with the candidate:

  • Head / Assistant Head of LFM: Leadership & final accountability. Oversees team operations, policies, team membership, appeals, major sanctions, and external department links (IFM, Property Management, Development).
  • Senior LFM Member: Middle management & guidance. Acts as mentor for Handlers, monitors workloads, handles Senior Admin tickets, No Mic requests, and approves internal faction structural changes and moderate sanctions.
  • LFM Handler: Direct faction oversight. Serves as direct contact for assigned factions, maintains RP standards and budgets, handles day-to-day requests on the spot, and processes single-faction dev requests.
  • LFM Support: Infrastructure & administrative foundation. Moderates faction Discords/forums, conducts document QA, assists handlers with daily tasks, case logging, and active ticket handling. Strictly restricted from approving requests, issuing sanctions, or altering policy.

Points of Contact & Faction Assignments

Direct candidate to channel #points-of-contact in the LFM Discord. Viewing this channel displays who is assigned to every faction on the server (Primary Handler, Support / Secondary Handler, and Overseeing Senior).

3. Tickets, Warrants, & Faction Leader Tickets

Primary Ticket Categories in Discord

  • Active Tickets (General Community & Player Inquiries): Range from simple questions to complex legal roleplay inquiries. Take tickets you are knowledgeable about, ask for help in #lfm-general-chat, and close them once resolved.
  • Faction Leader Tickets (Leadership Requests & Negotiations): Dedicated channel for faction leaders to make administrative or operational requests. MUST be handled by one of that specific faction's assigned Handlers.
  • Warrant Tickets (LEO Search & Arrest Warrant Scheduling): Streamlined process for LFM staff and volunteer Warrant Admins to schedule and coordinate warrant executions with LEO factions.
Mandatory Ticket Safety & Neutrality Rules:
• CONFLICT OF INTEREST WARNING: Never accept or handle an Active Ticket if you have a conflict of interest (involves your own character, personal faction, or friends). Pass the ticket immediately.
• FACTION LEADER TICKET ROUTING: Support staff must not claim or independently resolve Faction Leader Tickets. Always ping or defer to the assigned Primary or Secondary Handler.

5. Faction Flags

Flag Guidelines & Induction Explanation

During the induction, the mentor must explain how faction flags work, why factions need them, and when they are given:

  • What Faction Flags Are: System-level permissions and attributes attached via UCP that unlock core faction mechanics, equipment access, duty toggles, financial structures, and vehicle capabilities.
  • Why Factions Need Them: Without correct system flags, legal factions cannot access department radios, issue fines, spawn specialized fleet vehicles, manage faction bank funds, or utilize specialized government interfaces (like CAD or MDT).
  • When They Are Given: Flags are assigned upon official faction creation or approved expansion requests. They are strictly controlled based on faction tier, operational scope, and explicit LFM leadership authorization.
Direct candidates to #faction-flags in the LFM Discord for the complete itemized list of system flags and categories. Remember: never assign flags without explicit permission from LFM Leadership.
6. Legal Requests Panel

Active Legal Panel Request Types

No Mic Request, IC Discord Channel Request, Multiple Factions Request, Legal Money Grant, Passive Income Request, Legal Supply Request, Faction Report, Faction Application, Pharmacy Application, Gov Reform Application, Medical Licensing, Faction Vehicle Request.

General Evaluation Criteria (Request Filtering Checklist)

  • Alignment with Faction Concept: Does the request fit established lore and purpose?
  • Contribution to Roleplay Quality: Will this generate engaging, high-quality roleplay?
  • Balance & Fairness: Does this grant an unfair advantage over other factions?
  • Realism & Lore Consistency: Is it plausible in a modern San Andreas context?
  • Sustainability & Planning: Is there a clear long-term plan for utilizing this asset?
  • Track Record & Activity: Has the faction demonstrated compliance with server rules?
  • Interfaction Impact: How will this affect surrounding server dynamics?

Subteam Coordination & Workflows

  • Early Subteam Outreach: Contact RPQM and Property Management (PM) immediately when major assets/concepts are brought up.
  • Character Context Checks: Evaluate character wealth, portfolio size, age, and development time before approving major grants.
  • No Mic Requests: Restricted strictly to Senior LFM+. Evaluate medical/accessibility evidence thoroughly.
  • Faction Reports: Handled by assigned handlers under strict confidentiality. Never disclose the reporting party's identity. Redirect individual rule breaks to Forum Reports.
  • Medical Licensing: Reserved exclusively for LFM Leads+.
  • Faction Vehicle Requests: Check market pricing and precedent. Never issue emergency variants with bullbars/rambars to non-LEO factions. Follow Penal Code 1504/1505 regarding lightbars (amber-only for road presence/security). Never attempt to factionize admin-created temporary vehicles (`/veh`, `/acar`).
7. LFM Discussions

Primary Discussion Channels & Scope

  • Private Team Discussions (#lfm-private-discussions): Core internal channel for sensitive or faction-specific matters (applications, legal disputes, complaints, concept shifts, HQ relocations). Active monitoring and reasoned input are required from all team members.
  • Public / Broad Discussions (#lfm-public-discussions): Visible to broader staff and community representatives for large-scale systemic items.

Always launch a private discussion thread before making informal commitments to faction leaders regarding major moves, budget changes, or concept shifts. Support staff are expected to contribute objective arguments grounded in written policy.

8. CCTV & ESW Logs

Log Channel Tracking

  • #cctv-logs — Tracking camera installations, replacements, and property bindings.
  • #esw-logs — Tracking Emergency Services Weapons distributions, serial numbers, and audits.

CCTV Implementation SOP for Faction Properties

  1. Spawn Camera Items: Execute /agiveitem [your ID] [amount] [item name] (Options: 1080p CCTV Camera or 1080p 360 CCTV Camera).
  2. Toggle Admin Duty (CRITICAL): You MUST be on /aduty when placing cameras so they register as server-owned rather than bound to your personal character slot.
  3. Deploy & Collect IDs: Place cameras in approved locations and record all generated system Camera IDs.
  4. Factionize Assets: Forward collected Camera IDs to a Senior LFM member to bind them permanently to the target property.
  5. Public Logging: Post the detailed installation record in #cctvs using the pinned template.
9. Faction Types

Legal Faction Tiers Overseen by LFM

  • Unofficial Factions: Organized roleplay concepts a step above standard businesses that have formally applied for and received unofficial status.
  • Official Factions: Fully recognized legal entities providing public value (e.g., private media networks, security firms, public utility services).
  • Public Sector, Law Enforcement & Emergency Services: Government (GOV), Law Enforcement (LEO — Police, Sheriff, SADCR, SAPR), and Fire & Rescue (FD / EMS / Hospitals).
  • Secondary Factions: Labor unions, professional associations, bargaining units, and specialized trade guilds.

Maintain equal administrative oversight, financial auditing, and roleplay standards across all tiers.
